# Google Play Store Listing

## App name
Medicine Guide Pakistan

## Short description
Urdu & English medicine reference with brand, generic, uses and safety information.

## Full description
Medicine Guide Pakistan is a practical medicine reference app designed for quick access to medicine information in Urdu and English.

### Features
• Search medicines by brand, generic name, category or indication
• Urdu and English interface
• Medicine uses and common side effects
• Safety warnings and prescription (Rx) indicators
• Favorites for quick access
• Pakistan-focused starter catalog
• Offline access to the included catalog

### Important
This app is an educational/reference tool. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, prescribing or dispensing judgment. Always verify the latest approved product information, dose, contraindications, interactions and special-population guidance before clinical use.

The catalog should be reviewed against current DRAP information and approved product information before publication or clinical use.
