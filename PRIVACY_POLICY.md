# Privacy Policy — Medicine Guide Pakistan

**Last updated:** 2 September 2026

Medicine Guide Pakistan is an offline medicine reference application. The app is designed to provide educational/reference information about medicines.

## Data collection
The current version does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, phone numbers, contacts, location, photos, or health records.

## Local storage
The app may store language preference and the list of medicines marked as favorites locally on the device. This information is not sent to the developer by the current app.

## Internet
The medicine catalog is packaged with the app. The current version does not require an internet connection for its core medicine-reference features.

## Medical disclaimer
Information in the app is for educational/reference purposes only and is not a substitute for diagnosis, prescribing, dispensing judgment, or professional medical advice. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional and verify current approved product information before using a medicine.

## Changes
This policy may be updated when app functionality changes.
