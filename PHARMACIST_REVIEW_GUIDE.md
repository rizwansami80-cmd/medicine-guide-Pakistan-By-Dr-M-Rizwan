# Pharmacist Review Guide

Before publishing, verify every Pakistan-market brand/product against the current DRAP Registered Drugs Index and approved product information. Verify composition, strength, dosage form, registration number, marketing authorization holder, current market status and clinical wording. Add doses only when an exact indication/formulation and authoritative source are specified. Review contraindications, interactions, pregnancy/lactation and pediatric information. Remove or relabel any product that is not confirmed as current.

The app intentionally avoids hard-coded dosing because dosing varies by indication, age, weight, renal/hepatic function and formulation.
